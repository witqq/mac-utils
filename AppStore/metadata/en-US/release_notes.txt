Adds storage analysis with a saved, paginated folder tree, sorting by logical or allocated size, a largest-items list and an optional size map. Browsing and successful moves to the system Trash update the tree without rescanning. Whole-folder moves require confirmation; explanations and coverage warnings appear in the interface. A sidebar and resizable full-screen utility window make room for analysis. Display switching restores supported independent modes and remembered relative positions, including portrait displays. Storage access remains limited by macOS and native selection grants.
